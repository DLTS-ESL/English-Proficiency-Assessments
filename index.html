DOMAIN 1 · TEACHER READS ALOUD

1. Listening (25 points)

Listen carefully. Your teacher will read the passage twice. Answer the questions after listening.

Teacher only: Listening passage – A School Garden
Every Friday afternoon, students in Grade 4 visit the school garden. They work in small groups to take care of different plants. Some students water the vegetables, while others remove weeds and check the soil.

Last month, the students planted tomatoes, carrots and lettuce. They discovered that plants need sunlight, water, air and healthy soil to grow. Their teacher explained that insects can also help plants by carrying pollen from one flower to another.

The students are excited because they will soon harvest their vegetables and use them to prepare a healthy salad.

Read at a natural pace. Read the complete passage twice. Do not show the passage to the student during the assessment.

Listening questions

1. When do the students visit the school garden?

2. Which vegetables did they plant?

3. What do some students do in the garden?

4. What do plants need to grow? Name two things.

5. Why are insects helpful to some plants?

6. What do the students plan to do with their vegetables?

DOMAIN 3 · INDEPENDENT READING

3. Reading (25 points)

Read the passage carefully. Then answer the questions using information from the text.

Passage A: Why Do Bees Matter?

Why Do Bees Matter?

Bees are small insects, but they play a very important role in our environment. When a bee visits a flower to collect nectar, pollen sticks to its body. As the bee flies to another flower, some of this pollen is transferred. This process is called pollination.

Pollination helps many plants produce fruits and seeds. Without bees and other pollinators, some of the foods we eat would become much harder to grow.

However, bees face several challenges. Pollution, pesticides and the loss of natural habitats can make it difficult for them to survive.

People can help protect bees by planting flowers, avoiding unnecessary pesticides and creating safe spaces for insects.

Part A: Reading comprehension (10 points)

1. What do bees collect from flowers?

2. What is pollination?

3. Why is pollination important?

4. Name two challenges that bees face.

5. Give one way people can help protect bees.

Part B: Vocabulary in context (5 points)

Find a word or phrase from the passage that means:

1. The natural home of an animal.

2. Something that makes something else disappear or become lost.

3. The process of moving pollen between flowers.

4. A substance used to kill insects.

5. A substance that plants produce in flowers and bees collect.

Part C: Reading analysis (10 points)

1. What is the main idea of the passage?

2. Why might fewer bees affect the food people eat?

3. What is the relationship between flowers and bees?

4. Is the passage mainly informative, persuasive or entertaining? Explain.

5. What can you infer about the importance of protecting natural habitats?
